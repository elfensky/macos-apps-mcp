**Source:** `macos_apps_mcp/errors.py:92` and `adapters/mail.py:902`. **Apply to:** both new `files.py` raises. State what was refused, that nothing was written, what to do, and "Do not retry" where a retry cannot help. Use `e.strerror`, never raw `str(e)`.

### Module logging
**Apply to:** `files.py` FIX-10: `logger = logging.getLogger(__name__)`; `warning` for a leftover directory, `info` for a sweep.

**Source:** `tests/test_photos.py:620`. **Apply to:** every new test that calls `export_photo` through the client (it is absent under `MACOS_APPS_READ_ONLY=1`; CI runs the suite twice).
