The supplied synthesis and both GPT/Claude round files agree on public `provider_error_strategy="raise"`.
The restart research and both synthetic probes support a discovery-error classification change.
They do not establish late registration or prove the original November 1 incident.
The final consensus rejects a constructor-value pin test; behavioral failures detect a reverted default.
Retries, production readiness waiting, cache changes, private SDK patches, and replacement logs are excluded.

## Fixture choice

The existing stream fixture owns the disposable server, child cleanup, and short socket path.
Its optional generation mode adds a synthetic `doctor` and writes a generation-specific marker after Uvicorn startup.
The replacement reuses the outer fixture's directory, while each context reaps its own process.
The old socket deliberately remains after termination. The replacement waits for its own marker, not that socket or the old marker.
Normal stream smoke calls keep their existing tool catalogue and execution path.
Startup is bounded at 20 seconds. Teardown terminates for five seconds, then kills and waits for five seconds.
New requests have bounded test-only waits. Production transport deadlines are unchanged.
