## Tests

All three tests ran on the arm64 Mac (macOS 27.2) against the installed `develop` build
`3dd93b2 2026-11-06T15:02:36Z`. `doctor()` reported MCP `2.3.0` and FastMCP `4.1.6`.
The frontend was this Claude Code session and its own stdio shim. The owner approved the
restart and the outage on 2026-11-06.

### 1. Read-only device suite through the installed daemon
expected: Every read test passes. A read of a closed app skips and does not open the app.
result: passed. `uv run pytest -m device`: 19 passed, 0 failed, 8 skipped in 8.5 s.
Five write tests skipped without `MACOS_APPS_DEVICE_WRITES`. Three reads skipped because
Messages, Music and Safari were closed.

### 2. Daemon restart under a connected frontend (#543 item 1)
expected: After `launchctl kickstart -k`, the next tool call answers from the new daemon. It
does not answer `Unknown tool`.
result: passed. The restart replaced daemon pid 2052 with pid 45814 at 23:36:33 local time.
The new daemon bound its socket 2 s later. The next `doctor` call from the same shim answered
from the new daemon. A second call (`now`) also answered.
limit: The call arrived after the socket was bound. It did not fall inside the 2-second
startup window. The synthetic regression of 05.4-02 covers that window.

### 3. Daemon outage under a connected frontend (#543 item 1)
expected: A call while the daemon is absent answers with a connection error, without
`Unknown tool` and without the uncertain-outcome warning. The first call after the daemon
returns answers normally.
result: passed. `launchctl bootout` stopped the daemon at 23:42:06. A `doctor` call during the
outage answered `Client failed to connect: [Errno 61] Connection refused`. The answer had no
`Unknown tool` text and no "MAY HAVE COMPLETED" warning. `launchctl bootstrap` of the bundle's
own plist started pid 53656 at 23:42:23. The next `doctor` call answered from that daemon, with
every grant unchanged.
