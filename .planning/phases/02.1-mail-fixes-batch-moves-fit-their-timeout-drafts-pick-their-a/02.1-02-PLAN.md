<objective>
MAIL-03 and MAIL-04 in code, plus the #230 cap (D-13).

Spike 005 settled the MAIL-04 probe on device: Mail does not reject a `from_address` that no
account owns; it silently sends from the default account. So the refusal is mandatory (D-08..D-10):
one fail-closed read of every account's `email addresses` (`mail_addressing.owned_addresses`), one
check (`mail_outgoing.owned_sender`) that runs before the native write and never in a dry run.
`create_draft` gains `from_address` at four points (D-11), applied the way `send_mail` applies it.
`quoted_body` reads the original with a 120 s cap (D-13).

Purpose: a draft starts from the account the caller names, and no outbound path can silently
send as someone else (#208, #230).
Output: one PR rebase-merged to `develop`. The device checks are 02.1-06 (D-16); the facts entry
is 02.1-07 (D-18).
