# Phase 03.1 Plan 04: Contacts search fix Summary

The `contacts` search now reads id, name, organization, phones and emails with one Apple event each over the whole match list. The old loop re-ran the `whose` scan for every property of every person.

## Route

Route `a`, form `bulk`, no cap (`routes.fix02_route` = a, `fix02_form` = bulk, `fix02_cap_s` = null, from `p031-probe.json`). The route is not `unmatched`, so Task 1 asked the owner nothing and there is no "Owner ruling".

Measured on the Intel iMac (counts and seconds only): one broad scan 2 s; the shipped loop past 600 s (censored); the `get` form 12 s; the one-event-per-property form 9 s. The daemon's broad call hit its 30 s cap both on a fresh Contacts launch and after 10 minutes. Contacts was not blocked (V0 0 s).

## What changed

- `macos_apps_mcp/adapters/contacts.py`: new `_SEARCH` body. Five bulk reads, a length check on all five lists, a loop by index up to `maxN`, organization `missing value` becomes "", first phone and first email when present. The module comment now states the measured times and no longer calls the scan one cheap call.
- Unchanged: the `name contains q` predicate, the argv contract `(query, "50")`, `MAX_CONTACTS = 50`, the US/RS framing, `_parse`, `with timeout of 120 seconds`, the default host cap, `_VERIFY` and `_CREATE`. The #95 AddressBook plane is not built.
