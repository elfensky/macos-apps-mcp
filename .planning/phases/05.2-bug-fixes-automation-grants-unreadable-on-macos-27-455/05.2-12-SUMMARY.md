# Phase 05.2 Plan 12: doctor and Health show the Accessibility grant — PR #529, Part of #348

`doctor()` now returns an `accessibility` surface and the Health page an `Accessibility` row, both from the non-prompting `runtime.accessibility_trusted()` seam. PR #529 is rebase-merged to develop (rebased SHAs `a8d81dc`, `ce9173d`, `7800a91`; the lane commits were `3e76657`, `76afcf8`, `46d30de`). #348 and #516 are OPEN after the merge.

## What was built

- **doctor**: `_accessibility_surface()`, placed right after `_fda_surface()`. Gate first by `tiers.adapter_enabled("mail")` (`adapter_off`, ok None, no seam call). Then `runtime.accessibility_trusted()` called qualified: True gives `granted`; False gives `not_granted` (ok False) with `_AX_REMEDIATION`; `OSError` or `AttributeError` gives `probe_error` (ok None). The same single seam call runs for `request=False` and `request=True`; no Apple Event, no prompt.
- **Health**: `_PANES["accessibility"] = "Privacy_Accessibility"` and `_accessibility_row(report, active)` after `_fda_row`. It reads `_surfaces(report)` only. Granted is a green row; not granted with Mail on is a danger dot, the remediation and `Open Settings`; Mail off is a grey "so this does not matter" row; probe_error or a missing surface is a grey "Could not be read". `_denied`, `_refused_by_probe`, the banner and the sidebar dot are unchanged.
- **Tests**: `test_accessibility_surface_reports_the_grant` (4 cases), `..._is_off_with_the_mail_adapter`, `..._makes_one_seam_call_and_no_apple_event` (both modes, and the surface sits right after Full Disk Access), `test_no_module_binds_the_accessibility_prompt` (scans every package source for `AXIsProcessTrustedWithOptions` and `kAXTrustedCheckOptionPrompt`), `test_health_shows_the_accessibility_grant`, `test_accessibility_row_raises_no_banner`.
- **Docs**: DAEMON.md section 3 paragraph (bundle path, never a terminal, Mail in front about a second, clipboard borrowed and restored, refusal leaves nothing, doctor and Health report it); README Accessibility bullet; CHANGELOG `### Added` bullet.
