  aside, and only in the top-level INBOX. Reason: the Drafts autosave may keep the sent
  message's Message-ID (facts §4e `Send after insert`: "whether the sent message's Sent copy

  localized Drafts leftover pass as "delivered" and hide it. An INBOX never holds a draft.

  sets no logging handler, so INFO lines reach nothing (DAEMON.md: the unified log shows only
  what the process logs; no level is configured), and a WARNING for an expected outcome is
  forbidden (global rule).
- The probe keeps its convention (its own docstring: copies "inline so the probe runs as the
  bundle without importing the package"): `sig-check` carries small copies of the sweep's
  `_split_quote` HTML branch, `_bare` and `runtime._alnum`, pinned by its selftest.
- `server.py` and README.md are not edited: `drafts_copy_reason` is diagnostic; the result's
  `drafts_copy_note` stays the agent-facing text.
