# Phase 05.2 Plan 11: reply_all keeps Mail's own quote; the plain-text quote is removed

reply_all keeps Mail's own quote; the plain-text quote is removed — PR #534, Part of #348 (merged as dc66762, rebase-merge; #348 and #516 OPEN, #455 CLOSED).

## What was built

- `reply_all(dry_run=False)` runs the 05.2-10 flow (`open_reply_with_text(..., reply_all=True, send=True)`): Accessibility check, `_quote_facts`, `_OUTGOING_IDS` baseline, `_REPLY_OPEN` (reply to all, window), `compose_insert`, then `_SEND_OUTGOING`. `include_quote=False` is gui-replace (`replace_all=True`); `set content` is gone from the package.
- Route handoff recorded: `gap-route: a`, `gap-mechanism: M2`, `gap-no-quote: gui-replace`, `gap-cleanup: rollback-sweep`.
- Errors name the right tool (`reply_all needs Accessibility`, `reply_all did not complete`, `reply_all cannot confirm Mail's quote`); `mail_reply` text is unchanged.
- Docstrings (tool, `MailAdapter.reply_all`, `reply_all_to`, module), README, CHANGELOG (the #348 bullet rewritten in place) and facts 4e `Send after insert` say what the call does now.

## Post-send sweep design (owner decision 2026-11-01)

1. `_SEND_OUTGOING` answers; a failure (timeout included) raises the same exception class with `_SEND_UNSURE` appended (check Outbox and Sent before retrying); no discard, no sweep, no outbox read. A foreign exception is wrapped as `NativeError("send: <TypeName>...")`, its text never echoed.
2. `_ReplyFlow.after_send`: `window_gone()` stamps the time and takes the index top (`landed_by`) right after the send answers; `sweep(sent=True)` waits until 45 s after the window opened (`SWEEP_AFTER_S`), reads, and deletes only the one Drafts row proved by `find_own_draft` (empty `_OUTGOING_IDS` baseline, exact text + quote, row not above `landed_by`, a Message-ID, a mailbox named Drafts), by its own message id in its own mailbox via `_SWEEP_DELETE`.
3. `find_own_draft(..., sent_twins=True)` sets aside the sent message's copies: a threaded row in a Sent-named mailbox or Gmail All Mail with a Message-ID, the call's exact squashed text (optionally plus the attribution line) and the original's quote. Never claimed, never deleted, no `unproven`. Any other extra threaded row (other text, trimmed quote, localized Sent or Drafts, user folder, second Drafts row) keeps `unproven`.
4. The result carries `drafts_copy` (`removed`, `none`, `late`, `unproven`, `unavailable`) and `drafts_copy_note`. `sent` stays true; any sweep exception becomes `unproven`. `late` is the refusal rule applied to the send (a read under 30 s after the send answered cannot say `none`). Nothing in Sent or All Mail is deleted; nothing is deleted off the outgoing message after `send` (facts 3).
5. `Outgoing.dispatch` may now return a dict; `deliver` merges it after `with_outbox_pending` (own keys, so the outbox note is kept).

## What stayed of the plain-quote code, and why

Removed: `build_quote`, `quoted_body`, `_REPLY_ALL` (script + its tuple / `acting` / `_MAILBOX_SCOPED_SCRIPTS` entries), and their tests.
Kept (callers grepped first): `_ORIGINAL` (read by `_quote_facts` for the tail and attribution), `build_attribution`, `_stamp`, `runtime.mail_attribution_stamp` with its conftest stand-in, runtime and off-macOS tests (the sweep's ownership proof allows the attribution line), `ORIGINAL_TIMEOUT`, `sanitize_block`, `datetime`, the `_original_reply` test helper (fakes `_ORIGINAL`).

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 1 - plan vs §4e / owner rule] Post-send sweep added** (not in the plan): §4e `Send after insert` says the autosave copy survives a send and the call runs the sweep too; owner decision 2026-11-01. Recorded in §4e `Send after insert` and pinned by the new flow tests.
2. **[Rule 1 - conflict with the sweep] Plan item "delete `mail_attribution_stamp`, `_ORIGINAL`, `_original_reply`" not followed** — the acceptance grep for `mail_attribution_stamp` / `_original_reply` would print 0, not 1. They stay (see above); `quoted_body`, `build_quote` and `_REPLY_ALL` are gone from `macos_apps_mcp/` and `tests/` (one historical mention of `quoted_body` remains in facts §9, a device record).
3. **[Rule 1 - sweep design] Sent-twin recognition by mailbox name, not by Message-ID alone.** A Sent copy cannot be told from a localized-Drafts leftover by content, and a Message-ID match with the Drafts row would call the common case (autosave never landed, only the Sent copy exists) `unproven`. The name list is English like the Drafts rule (owner rule: stricter is acceptable; a localized Sent reports "may be left", deletes nothing). Row: §4e `Send after insert`.
4. **[Rule 1] Outbox read follows the sweep**, not the other way round: the sweep runs inside the one `run_native` closure before `deliver` reads the Outbox, so the Outbox count is read about a minute after the window opened (the settled queue). The sweep cannot change `sent`.
5. **[Rule 1] Tool-aware errors** (`reply_all` vs `mail_reply`) added to `_ReplyFlow`, the Accessibility refusal and `_quote_facts` (plan did not mention them).
6. **[Rule 3] RED commit also removes 16 plain-quote tests** and converts three of them to what the sweep still uses (`build_attribution`, `_quote_facts` with a US char, `_quote_facts` sender sanitizing) so no coverage of those functions was lost.
7. **[Process] The plan's PLAN_HEAD ledger (protocol 0c) was not written; `commits: 3` is measured with `git rev-list --count 23ff5fa..dc66762` after the merge.**
