## What changed

**Task 1 (tracer, FIX-16 G2 + FIX-14 note).** `runtime.accessibility_trusted()` keeps its name and dispatch. It now runs `tracked_run([sys.executable, "-I", "-c", _AX_CHILD], timeout=_AX_CHILD_TIMEOUT)` on the single worker. `_AX_CHILD` binds only the plain trust read through ctypes. A child that fails to start, times out, exits non-zero or prints other than `0`/`1` raises `NativeError` ("Accessibility check could not run ..."). No in-process fallback. `_ax_lib()` no longer binds the trust read. doctor's `note` is now "Grant each permission to the launching app (above). Automation and Accessibility need no restart; Full Disk Access, Calendars and Reminders may need one." Commits: 0a81292 (RED), 9f60970 (GREEN).

**Task 2 (FIX-16 G3 + docs).** `runtime._mail_pid()` reads libproc through new `_proc_lib()` (`proc_listpids` with `PROC_UID_ONLY` and this uid, then `proc_pidpath`). It matches the exact `_MAIL_EXECUTABLE` bytes. Zero matches: "Mail is not running". Two or more: "more than one Mail process". An unreadable list or path (size or second answer 0 or less, full buffer, unreadable paths without a match): "the process list could not be read". `ESRCH` paths are skipped. No AppKit; `_MAIL_BUNDLE` removed and its two test pins moved to `_MAIL_EXECUTABLE`. Docs: facts 4e `Accessibility check` row, `docs/DAEMON.md` section 3 sentence plus a new "Which permission changes need a restart" bullet, CHANGELOG bullet appended at the end of Unreleased `### Fixed`. Commits: 4fc3083 (RED), b374ff8 (GREEN), 71f400a (docs).

**Task 3 (to the draft PR).** Full gate, inline review of every hunk, push, draft PR #547. One review finding fixed in a456b82: my libproc fake class `_FakeProc` shadowed an existing `_FakeProc` in `tests/test_runtime.py` and broke two `terminate_children` tests; renamed to `_FakeLibproc`.
