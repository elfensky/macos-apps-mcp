<objective>
FIX-11 (#427): the dashboard names a Mail mailbox by account and folder, not by its
`imap://` URL.

- `mail_addressing.cached_account_names()` reads the Mail adapter's account cache without
  a native call; `mail_addressing.mailbox_label(url)` turns a mailbox URL into
  `Personal › INBOX`.
- The seven render sites of RESEARCH D1.4 use the label; the URL stays internal and in the
  raw audit lines.

Purpose: an operator can tell which account a batch moved mail from and to, and can read a
non-ASCII folder name.
Output: one PR rebase-merged to `develop` (`Closes #427`).
</objective>
