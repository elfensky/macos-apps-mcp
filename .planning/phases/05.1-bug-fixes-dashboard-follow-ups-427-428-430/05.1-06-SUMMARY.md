# Phase 05.1 Plan 06: Refusal states and dashboard accessibility Summary

FIX-12 part 2 of #428 (items 3 and 4). `html.page` takes `status` and passes it to
`HTMLResponse`. In `adapters.py`, `_render(request, *, restarting, status, notice)` shows one
`banner warn`: the notice plus `<a href="/adapters">Back to the switches</a>` when a refusal
gave one, else the standing lock reason. `toggle` answers the framed 409 for both lock
refusals and the framed 400 `Unknown action, adapter or value. Nothing changed.` for a bad
action; the posted value is never echoed. `_bar` lost its `locked` parameter. The bar shows
only while changes wait and no lock holds, and `restart` returns the framed 409 before the
hook and the audit write when `_lock_reason()` gives a reason. `guard.py` is untouched.

The switch `aria-label` is `{Label} adapter, on|off` and `aria-pressed` stays. The nine banner
glyph spans (five in writes, one in backups, three in health) are `<span aria-hidden="true">`.
`html.py` renders `<a class="skip" href="#content">Skip to content</a>` first in `<body>`, the
sidebar links in `<nav aria-label="Dashboard">` (footer outside), the four System entries with
`nav-item system`, and one `id="content" tabindex="-1"` target: the `.b-page` main, on the write
list the `.b-list` column, or the `.b-detail` main when a write is open. `dashboard.css` adds
`.skip` and `.skip:focus`, and inside the first phone block `.b-shell .sidebar nav` as a flex
row, `min-height: 44px` on `.nav-item`, `order: -1` on `.nav-item.system` and a transparent
`button.switch::after` hit area (`inset: -13px -6px`). Classes only; no inline style, `<style>`
or script (D-08).
