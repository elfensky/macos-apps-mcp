    (stdlib `calendar` and `datetime` only). Docstring: RFC 5545 §3.3.10 counts DTSTART as the
    first instance even when it does not match, and EventKit agrees, so this check only
    decides whether that instance is extra. Logic, in order, each failing filter returning False:
    - BYMONTH: `start.month` in it.
    - BYYEARDAY: the day of the year, or its negative form (day − days in year − 1), in it.
    - BYMONTHDAY: the day, or its negative form (day − days in month − 1), in it.
    - BYDAY: any entry matches. A plain entry matches the weekday. An ordinal entry matches
      when `start` is the n-th (or, if negative, n-th from last) such weekday of its month
      (MONTHLY, or YEARLY with BYMONTH) or of its year (YEARLY without BYMONTH).
    - BYSETPOS: build the candidates of `start`'s period — the month (MONTHLY), the year
      (YEARLY), or the Monday-to-Sunday week (WEEKLY; RFC default WKST=MO, and EventKit's
      `firstDayOfTheWeek` read 2) — keeping the days that pass the filters above. `start`
      must be at one of the 1-based positions (negative from the end).
    - Otherwise True: an absent part is implied by DTSTART itself. INTERVAL, COUNT and UNTIL
      do not affect DTSTART membership.
