<objective>
Card 2, part A (GATE-04, GATE-06): one registration record per tool. A single `_tool(tier, ...)`
decorator in `server.py` builds a `registry.ToolRecord` for every tool — gated-off tools included —
and the hand-maintained sets (`_WRITE_TOOLS`, `_SNAPSHOT_SOURCES`) become views over
`registry.TOOLS`. Audit verbs come from the record; `audit._audit_op` and its silent `"write"`
fallback are REMOVED (RESEARCH Pitfall: spike 2 kept `_audit_op` as a fallback — CONTEXT requires it
gone). Plans 01-12 and 01-13 continue on the same branch; the one card-2 PR lands in plan 01-13.

Decorator names stay (CONTEXT discretion "Card 2 decorator names"): `@_read_tool`, `@_write_tool`,
`@_additive_tool`, `@_send_tool` become thin aliases over `_tool(...)`, so CLAUDE.md, `.claude/CLAUDE.md`
and `tests/test_tool_annotations.py`'s vocabulary stay true. No `meta=` is added to `mcp.tool(...)`
(spike 2 did; nothing requires it, and it would change the published tool list).

Starting point: `server.py` as cards 1, 7 and 5 left it — the gates call `tiers.read_only()` /
`tiers.admit_send(adapter)`; notices live in `notices.py`. The outbound ledger stays in `tiers.py`
until plan 01-12 moves it into the registry.
</objective>
