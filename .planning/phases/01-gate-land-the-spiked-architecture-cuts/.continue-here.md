## Critical Anti-Patterns

| Pattern | Description | Severity | Prevention Mechanism |
|---------|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| Early requirement tick | The 01-01 executor marked GATE-13 complete, but ten plans carry GATE-13. The tick was reverted. | advisory | Every dispatch prompt forbids edits to REQUIREMENTS/STATE/ROADMAP. The orchestrator ticks a requirement only at its last plan (map below). |
| Plain `git pull --ff-only` in the main checkout | Local `develop` carries unpushed `.planning` commits, so ff-only fails after every PR merge. | advisory | Sync with `git pull --rebase --autostash`. |
| `requirements mark-complete` reformats REQUIREMENTS.md | The gsd-tools verb adds and removes blank lines across the whole file. | advisory | Tick with a two-line `sed` on the checkbox and the traceability row instead. |
| `state.begin-phase` on a resumed phase | It resets "Plan: N of 14" to 1. | advisory | Restore the line after the call, or skip the call on resume. |
