fingerprint still misses `size` and `message_references` — "out of scope here". Here it is in scope.

`message_global_data`, which would null out `message_id` — the column `query_sent_triage` joins on
and the #201 shadow view exposes. It must carry `message_id` across.

builds on them, it does not re-create them (RESEARCH "Note on timing").

Purpose: #180's convention — new `mail_index` reads are tested through the fixture — becomes the
default path, and schema drift on a read column surfaces as drift, not a query-time error.
</objective>
